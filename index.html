Our Story

A tale of two families

From Singapore to Seoul and back again, Hae San and Kristal's paths crossed where two cultures meet. What began as a chance meeting became a shared life — one that now brings together Chinese and Korean traditions under one roof.

Not at all — the RSVP lets you tell us exactly which parts of the day you'll join. The tea ceremony is an intimate family affair; the solemnization and banquet are for all our guests.
