Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation includes a plus one, we would love to have them — just share their full name and email in the RSVP so we can seat them properly.

Do I need to attend all three events?

Not at all — the RSVP lets you tell us exactly which parts of the day you'll join. The tea ceremony is an intimate family affair; the solemnization and banquet are for all our guests.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — parking is available at Raffles Sentosa. Please indicate in your RSVP if you'll be driving so we can plan spaces.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open half an hour before each event — arriving 20–30 minutes ahead of the solemnization or banquet gives time to be seated comfortably.

When should I RSVP by?

Kindly respond by 20 December 2026 so we can finalise seating and catering.
