Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need to attend all three events?

Not at all — the RSVP lets you tell us exactly which parts of the day you'll join. The tea ceremony is an intimate family affair; the solemnization and banquet are for all our guests.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — parking is available at Raffles Sentosa. Please indicate in your RSVP if you'll be driving so we can plan spaces.

What time should I arrive?

Registration opens in the Garden Room from 4:30 PM for the tea ceremony and solemnization, and cocktails begin at 6:20 PM ahead of the banquet — arriving 20–30 minutes before your event is perfect.
